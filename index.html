01 • INSTITUTION

About Nalanda College of Engineering

An at-a-glance profile of the institution.

🏛️ A Government Engineering Campus

Nalanda College of Engineering (NCE), Chandi is an engineering institution located in Nalanda district, Bihar. The information presented on this local portal is organized for students, parents, visitors and recruiters.

Established19 November 2008
Campus51.64 Acres
EnvironmentGreen & Wi‑Fi Campus

AffiliationBihar Engineering University, Patna
